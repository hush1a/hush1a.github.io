**Case Team Member** · UI Innovation War 2026 (Mini Case Competition)

**Methods:** Decision screening · System sizing · Business model & governance design · Project finance modeling (DSCR, stress tests) · Implementation roadmap

- Worked in a team of three (two cybersecurity students and one data science student) acting as advisors to the Community Investment Fund (CIF), deciding which of three communities should host its first community solar pilot and how to make it bankable.
- Built a decision screen across institutional readiness, logistics, anchor demand, post-subsidy debt coverage and replication potential. Recommended **Suka Makmur**, a rice-processing cooperative with the highest readiness (4.4/5), 1,100 t/year of committed throughput and 19 similar communities to replicate into.
- Sized the system against smaller and larger packages, choosing 350 kWp of solar with 600 kWh of storage: 81.6% solar coverage and ~109.5k litres of diesel displaced a year.
- Designed a hybrid SPV that separates ownership, operations and cash control: the cooperative runs the mill, an ESCO handles O&M, and revenue flows into a lender-controlled lockbox with a ring-fenced battery replacement reserve.
- Modeled an IDR 8.55bn blended capital stack (60% bank debt, ESCO and cooperative equity, CIF grant) that still covers debt service at 1.65x after the subsidy ends, against a 1.20x covenant, with stress tests, break-evens and a 16-month, three-gate roadmap to commissioning.
