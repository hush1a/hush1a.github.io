**Case Team Member** · BLCC (Semifinalist, Bank Jago case)

**Methods:** Problem framing · Product design · Credit risk framework · Financial modeling (NPV, IRR, NPL sensitivity) · Regulatory analysis (POJK 29/2024, PDP Law)

- Reached the semifinal with a 3-person team, answering Bank Jago's case on widening responsible credit for Indonesia's underserved, thin-file borrowers. Only around 18% of Indonesians borrow through banks or credit cards.
- Proposed **Jago Kredit Sehat**: a Financial Health Score built from consented behavioral data (spending, savings in Jago's Kantong pockets, cash-flow stability), shown to users with the factors behind it and concrete next steps. Users below the threshold get a financial wellness program instead of a loan.
- Designed a three-tier credit ladder, from Rp500k up to Rp50m, that users earn through their score and repayment record. Guardrails, a Credit Improvement Plan and SLIK registration help borrowers build a formal credit history rather than overborrow.
- Added a partnership layer that turns consent-based signals from the GoTo ecosystem, Bibit and Stockbit into health indicators, with anomaly and circular-transfer detection against score manipulation.
- Modeled three scenarios. In the base case the product moves from a ~Rp8.2bn loss in Year 1 to ~Rp54.6bn net contribution in Year 3 (NPV ~Rp24.9bn at 10%, IRR ~56%), stays profitable up to a 2.5% blended NPL, and is gated by a 2.0% NPL go/no-go threshold.
