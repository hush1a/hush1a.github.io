**Project Analyst** · Do Well Do Good UGM (ShARE client project)

**Methods:** Organizational diagnosis · SOP redesign · Balanced Scorecard · 360° evaluation · KPI design · Prioritization matrix

- Client engagement for D'Smile Dental Care with a 10-person ShARE DWDG UGM team. The final deliverable paired a marketing plan (SMILE) with an internal organization plan (CARE).
- Owned the organizational workstream, building **CARE** (Cultivate, Align & Reinforce, Evolve): a framework to keep service quality consistent as the clinic grows, and to reduce its reliance on the owner for day-to-day decisions.
- **Cultivate people:** designed a non-financial Staff of the Month program, chosen through KPIs, patient votes and evaluation, and a structured disciplinary system that grades violations on an impact × likelihood matrix with formal, progressive warnings, replacing undocumented salary deductions.
- **Align & reinforce:** rewrote the clinic's SOPs (registration, reminders, scheduling and rescheduling, decision authority) with an owner for every step, branches for out-of-routine cases, versioning and signed staff commitment. Backed them with visual cue cards, patient feedback forms, and a shared Google Sheets + Calendar workflow as one source of truth for appointments.
- **Evolve:** introduced a monthly Balanced Scorecard across financial, patient, internal-process and learning perspectives, reviewed in a monthly evaluation combining 360° feedback, manager review and a case-study session.
- Set organizational KPIs, prioritized the initiatives by effort and impact, and laid out the 3-month and long-term rollout.
